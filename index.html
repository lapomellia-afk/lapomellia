Our Heritage

The Art of Sculptural Form

Every creation from La Pomellia represents a harmonious dialogue between modern artistic sculpture and the precision of fine jewelry craftsmanship.

Our design language focuses on bold, fluid lines and organic shapes that resemble "liquid gold". This sculptural aesthetic ensures that each piece is not merely an ornament, but a wearable work of art that conforms beautifully to the human silhouette.

We use only 18K solid yellow gold, finished with exceptional high-polish treatments that catch and direct light beautifully across every hand-finished curve and plane.

The Standard of Excellence

Materials & Heritage

18K Solid Gold (AU 750)

Unlike vermeil or gold-plated jewelry that wears thin over time, La Pomellia utilizes exclusively 18K Solid Gold. This high-purity alloy ensures your piece retains its deep, radiant warmth and intrinsic value for a lifetime.

The Gu Fa Jin Aesthetic

Inspired by the "Ancient Gold" technique, our pieces feature a distinctive matte finish that is exceptionally silky to the touch. This artisanal process results in a subtle, sophisticated luster that distinguishes La Pomellia from mass-produced jewelry.

Hypoallergenic Luxury

By using high-grade AU 750 gold, we eliminate common irritants found in lower-quality alloys. Our jewelry is naturally biocompatible, offering absolute comfort for the most sensitive skin.
